Put on the goggles
------------------

Contributing used to start with hunting through repositories to find where something lives. The goggles skip that. Put them on and you see what the Future Traveler sees: every piece of HAX on the screen shows how it is made, who made it, and how to make it better, right where you found it.

<ddd-steps-list><ddd-steps-list-item title="X-ray"><p>Point at anything to see how it is made: its markup, its settings right now, and what it is built on.</p></ddd-steps-list-item><ddd-steps-list-item title="Makers"><p>Point at anything to see who made it. Follow a person and everything they built lights up, with their story on hax.cloud one click away.</p></ddd-steps-list-item><ddd-steps-list-item title="Flag"><p>Pick the piece and say what you saw: a bug, a feature you want, docs that need fixing, or something hard to use or see. It opens a ready-filled issue with a pointer to that exact piece and a screenshot.</p></ddd-steps-list-item><ddd-steps-list-item title="Translate"><p>Use HAX in your language and report wording that is wrong or still in English, in your own language.</p></ddd-steps-list-item></ddd-steps-list>

### How to put them on

1.  Open a site in the HAX editor. [browser.hax.cloud](https://browser.hax.cloud) works with nothing to install.
2.  Open Search and commands and run **Developer mode: goggles**. The goggles join the top bar.
3.  Press the goggles to put them on, then pick a lens.
4.  Reports go to the [HAX issue queue](https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues), so you need a free GitHub account to send one.

### Send someone straight in

Add `goggles` to the address of a page open in the editor and it starts with that lens on. The lenses are `code` (X-ray), `makers`, `flag` and `translate`. For Translate, add the language too, so a friend who speaks French lands ready to help:

See who built what you use
--------------------------

Every block carries the people who shaped it. The Makers lens and the stories on hax.cloud connect what you use today to the students, faculty and contributors who built it. When you fix something or add something new, your name joins theirs, and the next person to put on the goggles sees it.
