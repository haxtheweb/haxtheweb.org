Apache-2.0, the whole stack
---------------------------

*   No copyleft. Embed, fork or ship HAX inside your own product without relicensing your code.
*   An explicit patent grant from every contributor.
*   One license for the editor, elements, design system, both backends, CLI, MCP and skills.
*   Agent-safe. An agent can install, modify and redistribute HAX for you without creating an obligation you never agreed to.
