What this site keeps about you, in plain words.

Your choices

What this site keeps

Your choices are kept in this browser only. This site sets no other cookies, runs no analytics and has no trackers of its own.

Hosting

The service that hosts this site keeps ordinary request logs, such as the address a request came from, as every web host does.

Videos and other embeds

Videos from YouTube or Vimeo and content from other companies wait until you allow that company. Each one asks where it appears, and you can change your answer above.

What you send us

When you email us, post in the HAX Discord or open a GitHub issue, we use what you send only to answer you and to improve HAX. Discord and GitHub keep what you post under their own privacy policies.
