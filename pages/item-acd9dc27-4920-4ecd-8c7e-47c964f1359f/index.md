haxtheweb.org is the home of HAX: free, open web publishing you keep. It explains why HAX might be for you and every way to get it.

The HAX code is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, built in the open at [github.com/haxtheweb](https://github.com/haxtheweb). HAX grew out of work at Penn State, where it runs at [hax.psu.edu](https://hax.psu.edu/).

It is made by the HAX project (HAXTheWeb), an open-source effort to let anyone author, remix and publish on the web without needing to learn how the web works. This site is itself a HAX site; [its source is on GitHub](https://github.com/haxtheweb/haxtheweb.org). Its content is shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license.

To see how every part of HAX connects, visit [hax.cloud](https://hax.cloud/). To get in touch, use the [Contact](contact) page.
